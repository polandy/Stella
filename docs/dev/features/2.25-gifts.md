## 2.25 Gift ideas and gifts given **[M3]**

Two things are hard to remember and annoying to get wrong: what the household gave whom, and
when — so the same book does not arrive twice — and a good idea the moment it comes, so it is
there on the birthday it was meant for. Decided with the maintainer on 2026-10-06 and built in
three slices (#316, #329, and the hints and the palette entry of §2.25.5).

### 2.25.1 What a gift is

A gift belongs to **one person**, the one it is for (a couple's present is noted on each).

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | a short title, required: *Teapot, cast iron* |
| **Note** | optional: size, colour, where they saw it |
| **Link** | optional web address — the shop page. Only `http(s)` is kept; an address pasted without its scheme is read as `https://`; anything else (`javascript:`, `mailto:`) is refused |
| **State** | **idea**, **bought** (§2.25.9), **given**, or **received** (a present from them to the household) |
| **On** | the day a given or received gift changed hands; an idea has none |
| **Occasion** | optional: *Birthday*, *Christmas*, *Anniversary* — stored as a key and shown in the reader's language — or free text, shown as typed |
| **When** | an open gift only, idea or bought: *now* (the default), from an age, for a birthday, for a day, or *later* — §2.25.8 |
| **Visibility** | shared by default, private per record (§2.10), or *everyone but* the person it is for (§2.25.10) |

An idea and the gift it became are **the same record**: *Mark as given* sets the state and asks
only for the day and the occasion; title, note and link stay. A received gift is noted directly,
never from an idea. Who noted it is kept and shown as *noted by*; who *gave* it is not a field.

**Who may change what.** Whoever may see a gift may rewrite it, mark it given and remove it —
a present is the household's, and the member who buys the teapot is rarely the one who noted
it. Only its author may make it private, shared again, or kept from the person it is for
(§2.25.10), so nobody can hide a gift from the member who wrote it down.

### 2.25.2 The Gifts card

On the person page, after *Notes* and before *Mentioned in* (`'gifts'` in `CONTACT_SECTIONS`),
with its own jump-bar link.

- **With nothing noted** the card is one line (docs/dev/05 §5.5): *Gifts · No gift ideas yet.*,
  *+ Idea*, and the ⋯ menu holding *+ Given* and *+ Received*.
- **Tabs:** *Ideas* (open ideas, newest first; the tab carries their count, *Ideas · 3*, and
  the ideas still waiting for their time fold beneath under *Later · n*, §2.25.8),
  *Given* (newest day first, under uppercase year dividers) and *Received* (the same — the tab
  shows only once there is one). An empty Ideas tab invites: *Got an idea for Hilde? Note it
  here, and it is here when the day comes.* The card header has no count, so its jump-bar link
  has none either.
- **Adding:** *+ Idea* asks for the title; note and link fold away behind *Note or link*.
  *+ Given* (beside it) and *+ Received* (in the ⋯ menu) ask for the day (today by default)
  and the occasion as well. Saving needs only the title, and the day where there is one.
- **Already given.** While the title is typed — adding an idea or a given gift, or rewriting
  one — a title that looks like a gift already **given** to this person says so under the
  field: *Already given: “Teapot, cast iron” on 12 October 2023 (Birthday).* It looks like one
  when either title holds the other, case and accents folded (*teekanne* finds *Teekanne aus
  Gusseisen*), from three letters on; of several, the latest is named; the gift being rewritten
  never finds itself. Ideas and received gifts are not looked at, and nothing is asked of
  Stella — the card already holds every gift the reader may see (`alreadyGiven`,
  `src/lib/gifts/already-given.ts`). It is a hint, not a block: the second tin of her favourite
  tea can be on purpose.
- **Each row:** the title, its day and occasion, the note, *noted by …*, the link, *private*.
  An idea has no day of its own, so it says when it was noted instead — *Added 3 October 2026*,
  from the record's creation time, as the reader's calendar day. An idea offers *Mark as given…*, which opens the day and the occasion; every gift can be rewritten in place (pencil) and removed with
  the undo window every removal has (§2.23).
- **Offline:** noting a gift is a command (`gift.add`) and goes through the outbox like every
  addition (§2.18, docs/dev/04 §4.11.2) — kept on the device and shown on the card until sent.
  Rewriting, giving, answering *Still current?* and removing (`gift.edit`, `gift.markGiven`,
  `gift.answerWhen`, `gift.remove`) change what others may have seen, so they wait for Stella.

### 2.25.3 In the story

A given or received gift appears in the person's story (*Activity*, §2.23) on its day,
labelled *Given* or *Received*, with the gift icon and `--kind-gift`, its title linking to the
Gifts card and its occasion below. It is **read from the gift record**, a third source of the
merge, so editing or removing the gift changes the story too. Ideas never appear there, and the
story offers no removal for a gift — that happens on the card.

There is no touchpoint kind *Gift* any more (§2.25.4): the gift icon and `--kind-gift` belong
to the gift record alone, and *Log contact* offers *+ Given* on the Gifts card in its place.

### 2.25.4 Gifts from before

Before the gift record, gifts lived in two other shapes. Both become gift records once, so there
is one place for gifts:

- **The Monica gift notes.** The import wrote each Monica gift as a note titled *Gift* /
  *Geschenk* (id `<source>:gift:<monica id>`), its first line `🎁 **Title** — status, day`, then
  the comment and the link. Such a note becomes a gift: Monica's `idea` an idea, `offered`
  given, `received` received. The title, day, comment (as the note) and link stay — the link
  only when it is a web address, read the way every gift link is (§2.25.1); otherwise it stays
  in the note. The visibility, author and the note's own creation time stay too, and the gift
  keeps the note's id. Then the note goes.
- **The gift touchpoints.** Each touchpoint of the kind *gift* becomes a **given** gift on its
  person, on its day, keeping its visibility, author and creation time. Its title becomes the
  gift's title and its description the gift's note; with no title the description is the
  title, with neither the gift is called *Gift* / *Geschenk* in its author's language. A
  touchpoint with participants becomes one gift per person (a gift belongs to one person). Then
  the touchpoint goes, and the kind with it.
- **Nothing written by hand is lost.** A gift note somebody edited since the import, one whose
  first line no longer reads as the import wrote it, and one Stella cannot make a gift of
  (given or received without a day, a status Monica does not have) is left exactly as it is and
  named in the server log (docs/dev/07 §7.10). An original is removed only together with the gifts
  made from it, in one transaction.
- **When.** At every start, after the database migrations, and after every restore. Once nothing
  is left to convert, it writes nothing; the notes it leaves alone are named again at each start.
  Re-running is harmless: a gift takes its original's id (a participant's gift
  `<touchpoint id>:<person id>`), so an original that comes back — from an older archive, say —
  finds its gift already there and is only removed.
- **The story** shows the converted gifts on their old days, as *Given* or *Received* (§2.25.3);
  ideas never appear there.
- **Search.** A gift that was a note leaves the note index and is found as a gift instead, by
  its title and note, on the full search page (§2.9, §2.25.5).
- **The importer** writes Monica's gifts as gift records from now on, under the same ids. One it
  cannot make a gift of stays the note it always was; one whose earlier note was kept because
  somebody wrote in it is not written again.
- **Restore.** An older archive that still holds gift notes or gift touchpoints restores them as
  they are and the same conversion runs straight after, so it lands as gift records; the
  restore's report says how many gifts it made (§2.15).

### 2.25.5 Before an occasion, and from anywhere

- **Coming up.** A person in Home's *Coming up* band (§2.13.3) with open ideas the reader may
  see shows a third line under their row: the gift icon and *2 ideas*, a link to their Gifts
  card. Nothing new is sent or scheduled; the hint only stands where the occasion already does.
  The counts are one scoped query for the people in the band (`countOpenIdeas`).
- **The command palette** (docs/dev/05 §5.4) offers *Gift idea for …* next to *Write a moment*.
  It does not leave the palette: the field empties and asks *Gift idea for whom?*, the list
  becomes the people alone, and Enter on one lands on their page with the Gifts card's idea
  form open and the cursor in *What?* (`?gift=idea`). Backspace in the empty
  field, or the back arrow in front of it, returns to the palette's start. The idea is saved
  like any other — through the outbox when Stella is out of reach.
- **The search page** (§2.9) finds a gift by its title or its note, in a group of its own, and
  each hit leads to the person's Gifts card. ⌘K and Home's search field do not look at gifts.

### 2.25.6 Every open idea of the household

The *Christmas shopping list*: **`/gifts`** shows every open idea the reader may see, so the
member doing the shopping need not open each person. Decided with the maintainer on 2026-10-30.

- **By person**, one card each: avatar, name (a link to their Gifts card), how many ideas, and
  their **next occasion** — their next birthday, or Christmas Eve (24 December) when that comes
  first or no birthday is known: *Birthday in 3 days · 2 Nov*, *Christmas in 55 days · 24 Dec*.
  Whoever's occasion comes first is on top; people on the same day by name. An occasion 30 days
  off or closer is tinted. Ideas within a card newest first.
- **Each idea** as on the card: title, note, *noted by …*, link, *private*. *Mark as given…*
  opens the day (today) and the occasion, preset to the person's next occasion. The idea leaves
  the list at once, and the toast offers *Undo* for the same few seconds a removal has (§2.23);
  only then is `gift.markGiven` sent. It needs Stella, as on the card.
- **Ideas for later** are folded away (§2.25.8): *Show 3 ideas for later* under the heading
  brings them in, after the person's other ideas and dimmed; *Hide the ideas for later* folds
  them again. Each member's choice is kept in their browser, per member, so a shared tablet
  keeps two. The count beside the heading stays the open ones.
- **What is left out:** given and received gifts, and the ideas of an archived person (they
  stay on that person's page). Visibility is the central rule (§2.10): a private idea only for
  its author.
- **No nudges.** People without ideas do not appear — no *coming up, no idea yet* row; *Coming
  up* on Home already says whose day is near.
- **Reached from** the command palette's *All gift ideas* and the Gifts card's ⋯ menu. It has no
  place in the navigation (docs/dev/05 §5.5.13).

### 2.25.7 Not planned

Prices and budgets, wish lists the person shares themselves, and email reminders.

### 2.25.8 An idea for later

A good idea often comes years early: a bike with gears *from age 8*, a telescope *for the 10th
birthday*, a snow globe *for Christmas 2027*. Noted now, such an idea would sit among the open
ones for years and wear the list down. Decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-17: the time is
captured, the idea kept out of the way until then, and it comes back by itself when it is due.

- **When it is for**, chosen on the idea form as chips: **Now** (the default — open at once),
  **From age N**, **For the Nth birthday**, **On a day** (with *Christmas YYYY* quick picks for
  this year while it is still ahead and for the next), and **Later**. An age has a stepper
  starting one year above their current age. One plain line under the chips says what the choice
  comes to — *Turns 10 on 14 March 2028. It waits until 15 December 2027, then shows among the
  ideas.* — or why it cannot be kept.
- **Waiting.** An idea for an age or a day waits until **90 days before** its day — the birthday
  on which they turn N, or the day itself. A waiting idea counts nowhere: not in the *Ideas* tab,
  not on Home's *Coming up*, not in the brief, and on `/gifts` it is folded away unless the
  member asks for it (§2.25.6). On the person's card it stays in
  reach, folded under *Later · n*, soonest first and *later* last, each row with its chip — *From
  age 8*, *For the 10th birthday*, *Christmas 2027*, *Later*. With nothing open the tab says
  *Nothing due yet. The ideas for later wait below.*
- **Soon, then due.** From 90 days before its day the idea leaves the fold and counts again like
  any open idea — in the tab, on Home, in the brief and on `/gifts` — its chip saying how near:
  *For the 6th birthday · in 34 days*, *· tomorrow*, *· today* (*· about 2030* for an estimated
  year). From its day on it is due and the chip names only the time. *From an age* stays due
  for good: once they are 8, a bike *from age 8* never goes stale.
- **Occasion passed.** A birthday or a day that went by without the idea being given leaves it
  open and marked: the chip says *· passed* and the row asks *The occasion has passed. Still
  current?* Nothing is removed on its own. The answers: **Keep as idea** (it becomes a plain one,
  for now), **New time** (the picker, checked against today like any other), or **Remove** (with
  the undo window, §2.23). Until somebody answers, it stays where it is, still asking. An
  estimated year has passed only once its whole year is over.
- **The yearly look at *Later*.** An idea for *later* is looked at once a year, from 90 days
  before the person's birthday — or, for someone without a birthday's day and month, before the
  anniversary of the day it was noted. Then it leaves the fold, counts again and asks *Noted for
  later. Still current?* with the same three answers (*New time* can choose *Later* again). Once
  answered — or once its time is chosen anew in the edit form — it is not asked about again
  until next year's look.
- **Derived, never stored.** Where an idea stands is worked out from its time, the person's
  birthday and the household's day (`whenState`, `src/lib/when/state.ts`); the only thing kept
  is the day of the last answer (`when_checked_on`, docs/dev/03), so *later* knows its look is
  done. The command `gift.answerWhen` carries the answer (docs/dev/04 §4.11.2); like rewriting
  it waits for Stella.
- **An age needs a birth year.** Without one the age chips stay shut and the picker links to
  *Estimate age*, the person's dates editor (§2.13.1). An estimated year names no day, so the
  age is counted from 1 January of its year and said as *about* — *Turns 12 in about 2030*.
  Should the birth year later be removed, an idea for an age no longer waits: it is never lost
  unseen. A 29 February birthday falls on 1 March in a common year, as on Home. The idea keeps
  the age, never a worked-out day, so a birthday corrected later moves it along.
- **What is refused.** A birthday already over, a day already past, an age outside 1–150. *From
  an age* they have already reached simply holds now and is kept as *now*.
- **Rewriting** an idea keeps its time unless another is chosen; a chosen one is checked again
  against today. *Mark as given* clears it — a given gift waits for nothing. The age and the day
  live in `src/lib/when/`, pure and shared: an outing idea (§2.27) reuses the same rules and
  the same picker (`WhenPicker.svelte`), with its own forms — *in a month* (`in_month`) among
  them, which a gift does not offer.
- **In the calendar link** (§2.13.4), once soon or due: a one-off all-day event on its day —
  the birthday on which they turn N, or the day itself — *Gift idea: Bike (Mia)*, its chip as
  the description, linked to the person's page. It needs a day: an estimated birth year, an idea
  for *later*, one that is *now* or whose occasion passed stay out. So does any idea on the
  member's own record, so a calendar never spoils a surprise. Visibility is the feed's: a
  private idea only in its author's, an archived or private person's as on `/gifts`.
- **Not yet:** per-item visibility of the time.

### 2.25.9 Bought, not yet given

Between the idea and the day it is handed over the present sits in a cupboard. Noted only as an
idea, it would stay on the shopping list and somebody else might buy it again. Decided with the
maintainer on 2026-11-17 (concept *Geschenkideen für später*, E3).

- **Bought** is a state of its own between *idea* and *given*, reached only from an idea —
  the same record, title, note, link and time kept. *Bought* sits on each idea, on the card and
  on `/gifts`; a bought gift offers *Mark as given…* and *Not bought yet*, which makes it an idea
  again, as it was.
- **Off the list and the counts.** A bought gift leaves `/gifts`, the *Ideas* tab's count, the
  *n ideas* line on Home, the brief and the calendar link: it is no longer something to get.
- **Still in reach.** On the person's card it waits under *Ready to give · n*, below the open
  ideas, each row marked with the bag icon. In Home's *Coming up* (§2.13.3) the person's row
  says *Ready: Scarf, Teapot* until it is given — so the present is not forgotten in the
  cupboard. It is read with the same visibility as every gift (`readyGifts`).
- **Undo.** *Bought* and *Not bought yet* take the row away at once and the toast offers *Undo*
  for the same few seconds a removal has (§2.23); only then is `gift.markBought` sent. Like
  rewriting, it waits for Stella (docs/dev/04 §4.11.2). A given or received gift cannot be
  marked bought.

### 2.25.10 Everyone but the person it is for

A family where everyone is a member gives presents to members: the idea for Mama's birthday
must not stand on Mama's own page. Decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-17 (E9).

- **A third choice of visibility**, beside *Shared* and *Private*: *Everyone but Mama*. It is
  offered only when the person is a member's own record (their *This is me*, §2.10) and that
  member is not the one writing; a private gift cannot be one, and only the author may choose or
  clear it.
- **Kept from them everywhere.** The member it is for never sees the gift: not on their own
  card, not its *soon* or *due* chip or its *Still current?*, not in any count, on `/gifts`, on
  Home, in the brief, in their calendar link, in search, nor — once given — in their story.
  Everyone else who may see the person sees it as a shared gift, marked *hidden from Mama* with
  the eye icon. The rule is the central one (`canViewGift`, `giftVisibleTo`, docs/dev/03
  §3.7), so every read keeps it.
- **For good.** Given, it stays kept from them; the author can make it shared afterwards.
- **The archive** (§2.15) carries it marked (`hidden_from_recipient`), as it carries a private
  record: the admin's backup is the household taking its data out, not a member reading it.
- **One's own gift.** A member who notes a gift on their own record is refused the choice —
  they would hide it from themselves.
- **Offline.** `gift.add` carries the choice as `hiddenFromRecipient`; a command kept by an
  older device without it is a shared gift, as before.
