## 2.27 Outings with a person **[after 1.0]**

What the household wants to *do* with someone — *the zoo with Mia*, *a concert with Grandpa*,
*the lake by bike once she is ten* — used to live in a note, or nowhere. An **outing idea**
belongs to one person, says roughly when, and moves from *idea* to *booked* to *done*. Decided
with the maintainer on 2026-11-17 (decisions E1–E15). The German UI says *Unternehmungen*; the
code says `plan` (`activity_idea` in the database, commands `plan.*`).

**Not a calendar.** No times, no attendees, no reminders sent. **What is owed is a to-do**
(§2.26): *give the book back* is a debt, *the zoo* is a wish, and the empty card says so.

### 2.27.1 What an idea is

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | a short line, required: *The zoo* |
| **When** | optional, the shared *when* of `src/lib/when/` (§2.25.8): *some time* (the default), a day, a month, the birthday they turn N, or from age N |
| **Note, link** | optional; the link is an `http(s)` address, read like a gift's (§2.25.1) |
| **State** | **open**, **booked** (a date is fixed) or **done**; booked and done keep when and by whom |
| **Visibility** | *Everyone*, *Everyone but <person>* or *Only me* (§2.27.4) |

One idea is with **one person**. An outing with two people is two ideas — or one, on whoever
it is mostly about.

### 2.27.2 The Outings card

On the person page right after *Gifts* (`'plans'` in `CONTACT_SECTIONS`). The jump bar has no
link to it.

- **Empty**, it is the one-line card at the end of the page (docs/dev/05 §5.5): *Outings · No
  idea yet.* with *+ Idea*, and under it *Something you want to do together. What you owe
  someone is a to-do.*
- **Two tabs:** *Ideas* (counting the open ideas not yet booked, `openIdeaCount`) and *Done*.
- **Order** (`splitPlans`, `src/lib/plans/card.ts`): soonest *when* first, *some time* last,
  newest first among equals. Ideas more than 90 days ahead (`WHEN_LEAD_DAYS`) fold beneath under
  *Later · n*, as gift ideas do. A **booked** idea never folds: it is fixed, and stays in view.
- **Each row:** the line, its *when* chip (the gift idea's, §2.25.8), the note, then *booked* or
  *booked by …*, *noted by …*, the link, *hidden from …* and *private*. The checkbox at the
  row's **right** end ticks it done (docs/dev/05 §5.1); under the line *Booked* fixes it, *Not
  booked* takes that back. The pencil rewrites it in place; the bin removes it with the undo
  window every removal has.
- **Ticking** says *Done: …* with *Undo*; booking says *Booked: …* with *Undo*. A done idea
  moves to *Done*, showing the day it was done and *Not done* to open it again — a booking it
  had stays.
- **Adding:** *+ Idea* opens *What do you want to do?*, the *when* picker, *Note or link*, and
  the three-way visibility. Enter saves.

### 2.27.3 When

The *when* picker is the gift idea's (`WhenPicker.svelte`), with the outing's forms
(`PLAN_WHEN_KINDS`, `src/lib/plans/plans.ts`):

- **Some time** — the default; no day.
- **A day**, with the quick picks *Saturday*, *Christmas* and *Birthday* (`PLAN_QUICK_PICKS`).
- **In a month** — `in_month`, kept as `YYYY-MM`: *In May 2027*. Due on the month's first day.
- **At their N-th birthday** and **from age N** — as for gift ideas. Without a birth year they
  stay shut and the picker links to *Estimate age* (§2.13.1).

There is no *later* form: an idea far ahead folds by itself. What is refused is a gift idea's
(§2.25.8): a day or month already past, an age outside 1–150. Rewriting keeps the *when* unless
another is chosen.

### 2.27.4 Who sees it

- **Everyone** — the household, shared (§2.10).
- **Everyone but <person>** — shared, but kept from the member whose own record the person is
  (their *This is me*, §2.10): a surprise. Offered only on a member's own record, never by that
  member themselves. The idea is not on their card, not in its count, and a command naming it
  from them is answered as if it were not there (`canViewSurprise` / `surpriseVisibleTo`,
  `src/lib/server/access/`) — the gift's rule (§2.25.10).
- **Only me** — private, its author's alone, admins included.

Whoever sees an idea may book, tick, rewrite and remove it; only its author changes who sees it.

### 2.27.5 Offline

Noting, booking and ticking are commands through the outbox (`plan.add`, `plan.book`,
`plan.done`; §2.18, docs/dev/04 §4.11.2): kept on the device and shown on the card — a noted one
as a kept row, a booked or ticked one with *not sent yet* — and listed on Home's *Not sent yet*.
Booking and ticking are idempotent: who did it first keeps the credit, so one sent twice is
harmless. Rewriting, unbooking, opening again, noting again and removing wait for Stella
(`plan.edit`, `plan.unbook`, `plan.reopen`, `plan.again`, `plan.remove`, `plan.answerWhen`):
noting again is no replay, since a second one would note a second idea.

### 2.27.6 Kept with the person

Export and restore carry the ideas with their person (§2.15), *when*, booking and done
included; a merge moves them to the person kept (§2.2).

### 2.27.7 On Home

The ideas join Home's *Coming up* (§2.13) rather than a band of their own
(`src/lib/plans/home.ts`, read by `listOpenVisibleTo`, scoped like the card):

- **The next three dated ideas**, however far ahead (`COMING_PLANS`), sorted in among the dates:
  the title, *Outing with <person>*, and the day — *on Saturday* near, the date beyond half a
  year, *in March* for a month (sorted at its first day), *about 2027* for an estimated birth
  year. A date and an idea on the same day: the date first.
- **The person's date line** adds *booked: …* for a booked idea of theirs — which is then no row
  of its own — and *1 idea together* for the undated ones, both linking to their Outings card.
  An idea without a day is never a row on Home.
- **A passed idea asks** *Done? Still on?* for a week after its day — a month's last day, an
  estimated year's end (`ASK_DAYS`) — above the dates: *Done*, *Next year* (the same day, month
  or age a year on; `nextYearWhen`), *Keep without a date*, and *Delete* with its undo. The
  answer is `plan.answerWhen`, which also takes back a booking for the day gone by. After the
  week only the card marks it.
- **Kept close:** a private idea or a surprise shows only to who may see it (§2.27.4), and the
  folded line on a phone never names one (docs/dev/05 §5.5.1). A person deceased or archived
  brings none.

### 2.27.8 Afterwards

- **A moment about it.** The done toast offers *Write a moment* beside *Undo*; on the *Done* tab
  a done idea without one offers it too. It opens the story's composer (§2.22.1) on the person's
  page, starting from the idea's title on the day it was done (`momentDraftOf`,
  `src/lib/plans/afterwards.ts`); more people may be added as with any moment. A private idea or
  a surprise starts private, so the moment does not tell the member it was kept from; the writer
  may share it. Saving carries the idea in `moment.capture`'s `planId`, and Stella links the
  moment through `activity_idea.moment_id` (`linkPlanMoment`) — quietly not when the idea is
  gone, open again or not the viewer's to see, since the moment is saved by then. Deleting the
  moment unlinks it (`ON DELETE SET NULL`).
- **Out of reach**, the tick is kept on the device (§2.27.5), and the toast says the moment can
  be written once Stella is in reach instead of offering it: the link needs the idea done there.
- **On the card and in the story.** A done idea with a moment links to it (*The moment*, to the
  entry on the journal page); the link shows only to a viewer who may see that moment, too
  (`linkedMomentVisibleTo`, `src/lib/server/access/query-scoping.ts`). A done idea joins the
  person's Activity on the day it was done (§2.23), named for who ticked it, its title linking to
  the Outings card.
- **Note again** on the *Done* tab notes a new open idea for *some time* (`plan.again`): title,
  note, link, person and who sees it copied, the done one left as it was. No repeat rule.

### 2.27.9 The household's list

**`/plans`** lists every open idea the member may see (`listOpenVisibleTo`, scoped like Home),
grouped by when (`planListGroups`, `src/lib/plans/list.ts`): *This month* — what is due, what has
passed and is still open, and what falls in the current month —, *Soon* (1–3 months ahead), and *Some time* — the
undated ones, an age already reached, and an age without a birth year. Dated groups run soonest
first, *Some time* newest noted first; an empty group is left out. What is still more than 90
days ahead and not booked waits behind *Ideas beyond three months (n)* (a fixed label, `aria-pressed` carries the state), as on `/gifts`. Done ideas are
not on it; they stay on the card's *Done* tab. Each row ticks it off as on the card; everything
else stays with the person (docs/dev/05 §5.5.18). The Outings card's ⋯ menu and the command
palette's *All outings* lead here. Offline it is the page as last read, with ticks kept on the
device marked *not sent yet* (§2.27.5).

### 2.27.10 From anywhere

**The command palette** (docs/dev/05 §5.4) offers *Outing with …* beside *To-do for …*, the same
two-step pick: the field asks *An outing with whom?*, and Enter on a person lands on their page
with the Outings card's form open (`?plan=new`). *All outings* opens `/plans`.

**Full search** (§2.9) finds an idea by its title, note or link, **done ones included**: an open
one first, a done one marked *done* with its day, each leading to the person's Outings card. It is
scoped like the card, so a private idea or a surprise is found only by who may see it, and an
archived person's ideas stay out. The index follows rewriting and removing (docs/dev/03 §3.5).

### 2.27.11 In the phone's calendar

A member's calendar link (§2.13.4) carries **one entry per dated open idea**, on its lead day
(E15): 90 days before its day, or the day it was noted when that is later, so the entry stands
still rather than moving with today (`planLeadDay`, `src/lib/plans/calendar.ts`). It reads
*Outing ahead: The zoo (Mia)*, its description the when (*For 6 December 2026*), linked to the
person's page. The day itself is no second entry. An undated idea, one for an estimated birth
year, an age already reached, a passed one and a done one bring none; private ideas and
surprises reach only who may see them.
