## 2.27 Outings with a person **[after 1.0]**

What the household wants to *do* with someone — *the zoo with Mia*, *a concert with Grandpa*,
*the lake by bike once she is ten* — used to live in a note, or nowhere. An **outing idea**
belongs to one person, says roughly when, and moves from *idea* to *booked* to *done*. Decided
with the maintainer on 2026-11-17 (decisions E1–E15). The German UI says *Unternehmungen*; the
code says `plan` (`activity_idea` in the database, commands `plan.*`).

**Not a calendar.** No times, no attendees, no reminders sent. **What is owed is a to-do**
(§2.26): *give the book back* is a debt, *the zoo* is a wish, and the empty card says so.

### 2.27.1 What an idea is

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | a short line, required: *The zoo* |
| **When** | optional, the shared *when* of `src/lib/when/` (§2.25.8): *some time* (the default), a day, a month, the birthday they turn N, or from age N |
| **Note, link** | optional; the link is an `http(s)` address, read like a gift's (§2.25.1) |
| **State** | **open**, **booked** (a date is fixed) or **done**; booked and done keep when and by whom |
| **Visibility** | *Everyone*, *Everyone but <person>* or *Only me* (§2.27.4) |

One idea is with **one person**. An outing with two people is two ideas — or one, on whoever
it is mostly about.

### 2.27.2 The Outings card

On the person page right after *Gifts* (`'plans'` in `CONTACT_SECTIONS`). The jump bar has no
link to it.

- **Empty**, it is the one-line card at the end of the page (docs/dev/05 §5.5): *Outings · No
  idea yet.* with *+ Idea*, and under it *Something you want to do together. What you owe
  someone is a to-do.*
- **Two tabs:** *Ideas* (counting the open ideas not yet booked, `openIdeaCount`) and *Done*.
- **Order** (`splitPlans`, `src/lib/plans/card.ts`): soonest *when* first, *some time* last,
  newest first among equals. Ideas more than 90 days ahead (`WHEN_LEAD_DAYS`) fold beneath under
  *Later · n*, as gift ideas do. A **booked** idea never folds: it is fixed, and stays in view.
- **Each row:** the line, its *when* chip (the gift idea's, §2.25.8), the note, then *booked* or
  *booked by …*, *noted by …*, the link, *hidden from …* and *private*. The checkbox at the
  row's **right** end ticks it done (docs/dev/05 §5.1); under the line *Booked* fixes it, *Not
  booked* takes that back. The pencil rewrites it in place; the bin removes it with the undo
  window every removal has.
- **Ticking** says *Done: …* with *Undo*; booking says *Booked: …* with *Undo*. A done idea
  moves to *Done*, showing the day it was done and *Not done* to open it again — a booking it
  had stays.
- **Adding:** *+ Idea* opens *What do you want to do?*, the *when* picker, *Note or link*, and
  the three-way visibility. Enter saves.

### 2.27.3 When

The *when* picker is the gift idea's (`WhenPicker.svelte`), with the outing's forms
(`PLAN_WHEN_KINDS`, `src/lib/plans/plans.ts`):

- **Some time** — the default; no day.
- **A day**, with the quick picks *Saturday*, *Christmas* and *Birthday* (`PLAN_QUICK_PICKS`).
- **In a month** — `in_month`, kept as `YYYY-MM`: *In May 2027*. Due on the month's first day.
- **At their N-th birthday** and **from age N** — as for gift ideas. Without a birth year they
  stay shut and the picker links to *Estimate age* (§2.13.1).

There is no *later* form: an idea far ahead folds by itself. What is refused is a gift idea's
(§2.25.8): a day or month already past, an age outside 1–150. Rewriting keeps the *when* unless
another is chosen.

### 2.27.4 Who sees it

- **Everyone** — the household, shared (§2.10).
- **Everyone but <person>** — shared, but kept from the member whose own record the person is
  (their *This is me*, §2.10): a surprise. Offered only on a member's own record, never by that
  member themselves. The idea is not on their card, not in its count, and a command naming it
  from them is answered as if it were not there (`canViewSurprise` / `surpriseVisibleTo`,
  `src/lib/server/access/`) — the gift's rule (§2.25.10).
- **Only me** — private, its author's alone, admins included.

Whoever sees an idea may book, tick, rewrite and remove it; only its author changes who sees it.

### 2.27.5 Offline

Noting, booking and ticking are commands through the outbox (`plan.add`, `plan.book`,
`plan.done`; §2.18, docs/dev/04 §4.11.2): kept on the device and shown on the card — a noted one
as a kept row, a booked or ticked one with *not sent yet* — and listed on Home's *Not sent yet*.
Booking and ticking are idempotent: who did it first keeps the credit, so one sent twice is
harmless. Rewriting, unbooking, opening again and removing wait for Stella (`plan.edit`,
`plan.unbook`, `plan.reopen`, `plan.remove`).

### 2.27.6 Kept with the person

Export and restore carry the ideas with their person (§2.15), *when*, booking and done
included; a merge moves them to the person kept (§2.2).

### 2.27.7 Not yet

A later step brings a band on Home, a moment offered on *done* (`moment_id` is already in the
table), a household list, the command palette, search and the calendar link.
