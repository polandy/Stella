Removing a member

Settings gets a Household section with a Members page. An admin removes any other member there, after an inline confirm step. The member can't sign in any more, their shared work keeps their name, their private records stay sealed, and the household gets one stream line. Every control works: open Remove…, keep or remove. The switches pick the state, and the URL keeps it, so every PNG is a link.

What to look at

  1. Settings gets a Household section after You, with one link card: Members.
  2. The Members page lists everyone who signs in: you first, then by name, with the email, an Admin pill and how they sign in. Signed in as Nina (a member), there is nothing to press.
  3. Remove… is on every row but your own. It opens the sunken confirm step under the row. Focus starts on Keep Lukas. Switch the member to see the lines that only appear sometimes: SSO, and the last admin with a password.
  4. After the removal the row moves to Former members, with a toast and no Undo. Home gets one line that links nowhere. A removed member who signs in through SSO is turned away with a clear message.

Decided (2026-10-10)

  1. The account is marked removed, not deleted. Sessions and API tokens go; the SSO identity and the password hash stay, never checked while removed.
  2. Private records stay sealed: nobody sees them, admins included.
  3. Their name stays on everything they wrote. They leave the lists of current members.
  4. Confirmed and logged, no Undo toast. Restore on Former members comes in slice 2.
