Write a moment without leaving the page

Today Write a moment on a person’s page goes to /contacts/{id}/journal, although the Activity card that shows the moment is on this very page. Here the button opens the moment composer inline, at the top of the Activity card: the page scrolls there, the field is focused, and the saved moment appears in the timeline. The journal page stays — Open journal in the card’s header. Everything below is clickable.

What changes

  1. The button stays where it is — the primary in the identity card. Pressed, the page glides to the Activity card, the composer unfolds at its top (the §5.11 reveal) and the cursor is in the field. On a phone the keyboard comes up and the composer sits just under the top bar, above it. Pressing it again while open only brings you back to the field.
  2. The same composer as Home — one serif field, Shared, the day pill (Today; a tap offers the last week), Photo, Save (⌘↵). What differs: the moment already belongs to this person, so no @ is needed for them, and @ offers everyone else — as on the journal page. The anchor shows as a chip above the field (switch Anchor to compare without it).
  3. Save folds the composer away and the moment appears at the top of the timeline under today, lit for a moment, with the usual Saved toast (for someone with nothing yet, like Kurt, the empty state steps aside while the composer is open). A second moment the same day joins today’s entry as a new paragraph (§2.20’s day slot) — try saving twice. While Stella is out of reach it is kept on the device and shows above the timeline, as kept logs already do.
  4. Cancel (or Esc) folds it away. If something was typed, the toast offers Undo, which reopens it with the text — nothing is lost to a stray tap.
  5. Open journal joins Log contact in the card’s header and leads to the journal page as it is: the long-form reading view, where a moment is edited or deleted.
  6. Log contact already opens in this spot today. Proposed: both forms share it, one at a time — opening one folds the other away, keeping what was typed. Switch Log contact to see the alternative: one composer with a Moment · Contact switch.
