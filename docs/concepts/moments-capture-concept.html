Moments: capture a memory in one sentence

Monica asks you to fill in modules. Stella asks you what happened. One field on Home turns "met Julia at the lake, she's @Marco's sister" into a person, a journal entry and a household update — without leaving the page. The two prototypes below are live: type in them.

Mentions do the linking

The existing @-mention parser and picker are reused. A mention that matches nobody offers "Create …" inline, which is the quick-add form reduced to its one required field.

1

The first person is the anchor

A journal entry needs one contact today. The first mention becomes that contact; the others are stored as mentions, exactly as the journal already does. No schema change for the MVP.

?

Relationships are offered, not parsed

"She's Marco's sister" is not interpreted. After saving, a quiet hint asks whether to link the two people; one tap opens the relationship picker pre-filled. Wrong guesses cost nothing.

◐

Private stays private

The visibility chip mirrors the journal. A private moment appears only in its author's stream, marked with a lock, and never in anyone else's.

▤

The stream is a query, not a table

New people, new relationships and new moments come from the existing tables, ordered by time and filtered through the central visibility rules. Nothing is logged twice.

Scope

What the MVP contains

In

  • Capture field on Home and as a ⌘K action, with @-autocomplete and inline create.
  • Household stream: moments, new people, new relationships, newest first, visibility-scoped.
  • Follow-up hint after a moment with two or more people: "Link A and B?".
  • Photo attach using the existing browser-side processing.
  • Mobile composer above the tab bar.

Reused as is

  • Journal entries, day slots and visibility.
  • @-mention parser, resolver and picker.
  • Quick-add contact use-case.
  • Avatars, relative timestamps, app shell.

Deliberately later

  • Parsing relationships out of the sentence.
  • Moments without any person ("family trip").
  • Stream filters by member or type.
  • Interaction kinds (call, visit, gift).
  • Reactions or comments. This is not a social network.
