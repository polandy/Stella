What changes

  • A1Add person goes quiet. An outlined icon button; the filled primary is left to the page’s own action (the pencil on Home, Write in journal on a person).
  • B1One Filter pill. Four lines of chips become a pill with the active count; What and Who open in a sheet.
  • B2One search door on Home. The field is the search; the top-bar icon stays on every other page.
  • C10Back link, not a breadcrumb. “‹ People” never wraps; the name is the card’s headline anyway.
  • C4The description wraps to two lines instead of being cut off with an ellipsis.
  • C5No “Last contact” until there is one. The facts hold only what the record has.
  • C2Empty cards shrink. Photos and Notes become one line each, Activity keeps one sentence, Mentioned in is not shown at zero.

The chips stay links (?kind=, ?by=), so the URL contract and the no-JS path are unchanged; from md the rows stay as they are.

Switch to Sandra Brunner-Keller to see the breadcrumb wrap in Before. The jump bar keeps Photos and Notes: that is where their add buttons are.
