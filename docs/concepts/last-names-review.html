Rules in one look

  1. Not Brunner drops one name for one person (household-wide). The person stays.
  2. No last name settles the person: off the list, off the counts, no chip on the profile, no pass-on offers. A last name given later clears it.
  3. Both are undone in the toast for 8 s, and later in their drawer.
