Photos of a circle, by role

The mockup for docs/concepts/circle-photos.md. A circle gets a gallery. A photo without a role becomes the circle's cover, and a photo with a role sits as a banner above that role's group. In both cases the favourite leads, otherwise the newest. The pictures are drawn placeholders.

Class 1B with a cover and a banner over Student. Teacher has no photo, so that group looks as it does today. The Photos section sits below the members.
