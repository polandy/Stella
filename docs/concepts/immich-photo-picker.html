From Immich, beside every upload

A journal entry, a moment, a person's and a circle's gallery each get From Immich. The picker opens on the entry's day: every photo Immich has from that day, whoever is in it. Smart search runs across the whole library. Several photos can be ticked at once, and each one is copied into Stella (downscaled, EXIF/GPS stripped). Tap through the states below. The URL keeps them (?place=moment&state=day&device=phone&theme=dark).
