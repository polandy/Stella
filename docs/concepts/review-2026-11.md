## Server — domain, access, db

Scope: `src/lib/server/{domain,access,db}`, `drizzle/`. Read-only. The access layer itself
(`access/visibility.ts`, `access/query-scoping.ts`) is sound: every predicate has its SQL twin,
reads in every adapter join the parent contact and go through `childRecordVisibleTo` /
`contactVisibleTo`, and audit rows are written in the same transaction as the change. The
findings below are the places where the surrounding code does not keep that standard.

### SV-01 — Admin-only delete and merge are enforced only at the edge, and docs/03 §3.7 says otherwise
- **Severity:** medium · **Effort:** S · **Kind:** security
- **Where:** `src/lib/server/domain/contacts/remove-contact.ts:144-171`, `src/lib/server/domain/contacts/remove-contact.ts:181-213`, `src/routes/(app)/contacts/[id]/actions/record.ts:3`
- **Problem:** docs/02 §2.2 makes merging and deleting a person admin-only, but neither `deleteContact` nor `mergeContacts` nor `contact-repository.ts` / `contact-merge.ts` asks for a role: the comment at line 144 says "*Who* may delete is decided at the edge", and the only gate is `requireAdmin` in the form action. Every other role-aware rule (`canRemoveAuthored`, `canDeleteCircle`, `canRemoveMember`) lives in `access/` with a `Remover`, and docs/03 §3.7 states that the remove-authored rule is "the only rule here that looks at the role, beside who removes a member" — which is now false. A second caller (a command handler, the import API, a future bulk tool) gets no refusal from the domain.
- **Proposal:** give `access/visibility.ts` a `canDeleteContact(remover)` / `canMergeContacts(remover)` (role === 'admin') with the matching SQL in `query-scoping.ts`, take a `Remover` in `deleteContact`/`mergeContacts`, and list it in docs/03 §3.7.
- **Confidence:** confirmed

### SV-02 — Merging a shared person into a private survivor hides other members' shared records
- **Severity:** medium · **Effort:** M · **Kind:** data-loss
- **Where:** `src/lib/server/db/contact-merge.ts:227-253`, `src/lib/server/domain/contacts/remove-contact.ts:199-209`, `src/lib/server/domain/contacts/merge-plan.ts:57-93`
- **Problem:** the transaction only requires both records to be visible to the admin (`contactVisibleTo`) and then repoints every child row to `keepId`. When the survivor is the admin's *private* contact and the merged-away one is shared, every note, interaction, photo, gift, to-do and journal entry other members wrote about the shared person now hangs off a contact they cannot see (`childRecordVisibleTo` requires the parent visible), and the `contact.merged` log row carries `pair.keep.visibility` = private, so the household is not even told their records went away. Docs/02 §2.2 says only that "the survivor keeps their display name and their visibility"; it does not say the household's records vanish from everyone else's view.
- **Proposal:** refuse the merge (a `TranslatableError`) when the survivor is private and the merged record is shared, or require the admin to make the survivor shared first; alternatively write the audit row with the *least* private of the pair and document the consequence.
- **Confidence:** plausible

### SV-03 — The export reads four tables the archive never carries, with comments promising a restore benefit
- **Severity:** medium · **Effort:** S · **Kind:** docs-drift
- **Where:** `src/lib/server/db/archive-repository.ts:72-90`, `src/lib/server/domain/archive/document.ts:131-410`
- **Problem:** `EXPORTED_TABLES` lists `immich_name_ignore`, `contact_source_link`, `suggestion_dismissal` and `command_receipt`; the comments on `suggestion_dismissal` ("Left out, a restore would re-offer every suggestion they have already said no to") and `command_receipt` ("a phone still holding unsent items could send them twice") describe why they *must* be carried. `buildArchiveDocument` never reads any of the four (`t('…')` is called for 24 tables; none of these), so the rows are read, held in memory and dropped, and a restore does re-offer every declined claim and does accept a duplicate resend. `EXCLUDED_TABLES` does not mention them either, so the completeness test passes while the backup is silently incomplete.
- **Proposal:** either carry the four tables in `document.ts` (and `restore.ts`), or move them to `EXCLUDED_TABLES` with an honest reason and drop the reads; in both cases fix the comments and docs/02 §2.15.
- **Confidence:** confirmed

### SV-04 — Full search still returns notes, moments, gifts and to-dos of archived people
- **Severity:** medium · **Effort:** S · **Kind:** bug
- **Where:** `src/lib/server/db/search-repository.ts:68-143`
- **Problem:** contacts are searched with `contactBrowsableBy` (adds `archived_at IS NULL`), but the note, moment, gift and to-do searches scope the joined contact with `childRecordVisibleTo` only, so an archived person keeps turning up in search through every record about them. Docs/02 §2.2 says archiving takes a person out of the lists and the search, and the to-do / gift list adapters already add `contactBrowsableBy` for exactly that reason (`todo-repository.ts:98-99`, `gift-repository.ts:122-123`).
- **Proposal:** add `contactBrowsableBy(viewer)` to the four child-record searches (or make a `childRecordBrowsableBy` in `query-scoping.ts` and use it in search, stream and the two list adapters).
- **Confidence:** plausible

### SV-05 — `editProfile` writes back a stale `displayName`
- **Severity:** low · **Effort:** S · **Kind:** bug
- **Where:** `src/lib/server/domain/contacts/contacts.ts:308-323`, `src/lib/server/db/contact-repository.ts:219-228`
- **Problem:** `editProfile` reads the contact, then calls `updateProfile` with `displayName: contact.displayName` although the use-case only changes the description ("the name is kept as stored"). Two members editing at once — one renaming through `editNameParts`, one rewording the description — lose the rename, because the second write puts the old name back.
- **Proposal:** drop `displayName` from `ProfilePatch` and have `updateProfile` set `description` and `updatedAt` only.
- **Confidence:** confirmed

### SV-06 — Contact writes are by bare id after a separate visibility read
- **Severity:** low · **Effort:** M · **Kind:** security
- **Where:** `src/lib/server/db/contact-repository.ts:210-252`, `src/lib/server/domain/contacts/contacts.ts:314-437`
- **Problem:** `setArchived`, `updateProfile`, `setGender`, `setJob`, `writeNames` and `markWithoutLastName` update `WHERE id = ?` and rely on the caller having called `findByIdVisibleTo` a moment earlier. The note, interaction, photo, relationship and circle adapters put the rule into the statement itself (`inArray(id, <scoped subquery>)`), which the file comments call out as the pattern. Here the check and the write are two statements with no transaction, so a contact made private between them is still written, and any new caller can skip the read.
- **Proposal:** take the `Viewer` in these writes and scope the `UPDATE` with `contactVisibleTo` (return the affected count), as `note-repository.ts:111-123` does.
- **Confidence:** plausible

### SV-07 — The generation-contradiction guard has no database backing
- **Severity:** low · **Effort:** S · **Kind:** bug
- **Where:** `src/lib/server/domain/relationships/relationships.ts:462-475`, `src/lib/server/db/relationship-repository.ts:63-81`
- **Problem:** a duplicate link is caught twice — `exists()` and the `relationship_unique` index — but the "same generation claimed both ways" rule is only the `exists(to, from, typeId)` read before `insert`. Two members adding *parent of* in opposite directions at the same time both pass the read and both rows land; nothing refuses the second. The picker offering both sides from one screen is exactly the case the comment names.
- **Proposal:** put the pair check inside the insert's transaction (read + insert in one `db.transaction`, as `removeAllVisibleTo` does) or add a partial unique index on `(min(from,to), max(from,to), type_id)` for generation types.
- **Confidence:** plausible

### SV-08 — Domain refusals of user input thrown as plain `Error`, not a `Phrase`
- **Severity:** low · **Effort:** S · **Kind:** maintainability
- **Where:** `src/lib/server/domain/relationships/relationships.ts:453`, `src/lib/server/domain/relationships/relationships.ts:621`, `src/lib/server/domain/circles/circles.ts:352`, `src/lib/server/domain/homes/homes.ts:262`, `src/lib/server/domain/contact-fields/contact-fields.ts:85-119`, `src/lib/server/domain/journal/journal.ts:336`
- **Problem:** docs/08 §8.7 says domain errors carry a `Phrase`. These throw `new Error('Unknown relationship type.')`, `'A circle needs a name.'`, `'An address needs a line.'`, `'A journal entry needs some content.'` etc. for conditions a member can cause. Through `dispatchCommand` (`domain/commands/dispatch.ts:189-196`) anything that is not a `TranslatableError` becomes a `CommandFailedError`, i.e. a 500 and a bug report instead of a refusal the phone can show. Today the wire edge (`commands/parse.ts`) pre-validates most of them, so the paths are hard to reach, but the type id in `relationship.add` is only checked for being a string.
- **Proposal:** make each a `TranslatableError` with an `errors.*` phrase (there is one for most already, e.g. `errors.relationship.badSuggestion`).
- **Confidence:** confirmed

### SV-09 — Restore reports rows dropped for a missing parent as "already here"
- **Severity:** low · **Effort:** S · **Kind:** bug
- **Where:** `src/lib/server/db/restore-repository.ts:226-233`, `src/lib/server/domain/archive/import.ts:37-41`
- **Problem:** `skipped = rows.length - added` lumps together rows `INSERT OR IGNORE` left alone (already here) and rows `isWritable` never attempted (parent missing, journal day slot taken, cycle). `TableOutcome.skipped` is documented and shown to the admin as "how many were already here", so a restore that silently loses a member's journal entries (the case the long comment at 188-199 describes) reports them as present.
- **Proposal:** count the `continue` branch separately and surface it as a `RestoreWarning` (`rowsWithoutParent`, per table) in the report.
- **Confidence:** confirmed

### SV-10 — `importArchive` leaves the rows in but the log row and media out when a later step fails
- **Severity:** low · **Effort:** S · **Kind:** data-loss
- **Where:** `src/lib/server/domain/archive/import.ts:133-173`, `src/lib/server/db/restore-repository.ts:250-252`
- **Problem:** `applyRestore` is one transaction, but `convertHeldGifts`, every `media.put` and `recordImport` run after it, each on its own. A full disk during the media loop or a failure in the gift conversion throws out of the use-case with the rows already restored and no `archive.restored` line — the one case the comment in `archive.ts:14-17` says the log exists for ("the household sees that it happened"). Every other audit in the codebase is written inside the change's transaction (ADR-048).
- **Proposal:** have `applyRestore` take the audit entry and insert it in its transaction (as `deleteRemovableBy` and `mergeContacts` do); report media failures as warnings rather than throwing after the rows are in.
- **Confidence:** confirmed
