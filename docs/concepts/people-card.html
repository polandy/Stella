UX review · C8 + C9 · mockup

The People card: one map pattern on every width, a fold that says what it hides

C8 — the phone's preview-and-enlarge map on desktop too: a short strip across the card's top that grows in place, and the list gets the full card width (four columns). The alternative keeps today's column and draws names in it. C9 — the fold shows the entered groups first; the worked-out relatives wait entirely behind Show more, and a collapsed line names every group the fold hides (Friends · 3 · Work · 2). Everything is clickable: the map icons, Show more, and the groups on the collapsed line. ★ marks the recommendation.

Data is the demo seed (`SEED_DEMO`). Markus: 10 entered + 5 worked out. Sandra: 12 entered in three groups + 3 worked out. Daniel: 5 entered + 4 worked out — the case where only worked-out people are left behind the fold. Kurt: 3 entered — nothing folds, nothing changes but the strip. The identity card is abbreviated.

What changes

  1. Desktop map → strip. A 7.5 rem strip across the card's top, under the header: the household in a wide fan with first names (wide enough to label, unlike the 20 rem column). The two icons in its corner are the phone's: Enlarge map grows the strip in place to 24 rem and cross-fades to the live explorer (filter, arrange, shrink, full screen); Full screen opens it full screen. Same view machine as the phone (phone-map.ts), now on every width.
  2. List at full width. Four columns on a card from 48 rem (two on a phone, three in between) — Sandra Brunner-Keller no longer wraps.
  3. Phone: map unchanged. Its 100 px preview already is the pattern.
  4. Fold order. Folded, the card shows the first six entered people in group order; the worked-out relatives no longer take two of the folded places — they sit entirely behind Show more. Unfolded, nothing changes.
  5. The collapsed line. Under the folded tiles, one line names every group the fold hides with its count, the worked-out block last: Friends · 3 · Also related · 5. A group that is partly shown (Family · 7 with six tiles) is not repeated — its heading already counts it. German: Freunde · 3 · Arbeit · 2 · Auch verwandt · 5.
  6. Rules stay pure in people-groups.ts (foldPeople returns the hidden groups; the derived count folds with the entered one) — test-first.
