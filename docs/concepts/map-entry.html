The map entry: an honest way into the map, not a fake one

The drawn preview (ring on a phone, fan wider up) goes. In its place a compact strip that does not pretend to be a map: the faces of the direct relations, the title, and the two counts the live map will show — direct and within two steps, the same reach as the explorer. The whole strip is Enlarge map; the icon at its end is Full screen. Tap the strip in either frame to grow the live map in the card (here a placeholder), then Shrink map in its toolbar.

Data is the demo seed (Markus Brunner, 10 entered). Within two steps counts everyone the live map shows around this person — the direct ones included — so the strip's second number is exactly the number of faces on the enlarged map. Faces stand in for photos as silhouettes; No photos shows the initials fallback. The identity card is abbreviated.

The variants

  1. A · Faces + title + counts (recommended). One row: a stack of the first direct relations (four, three on a phone) with a +N chip, then Relationship map over 10 direct · 24 within two steps, then the enlarge glyph and the full-screen icon. Says who, what and how many — and that it is a door, not a drawing.
  2. B · A plus category dots. The same row with a third line: a coloured dot and count per group (family, friends, work), in the category colours the map uses for its lines. More information, but it repeats the group headings of the list right below, and three lines make the strip taller on a phone.
  3. C · A list row. Map icon, Relationship map, one muted count (24 people) and a chevron that turns as the map opens. The quietest; but no faces means nothing to recognise, and it reads like a link to another page.

What stays

  1. Enlarge in the card is unchanged: the frame glides from the strip's height to the enlarged one (100dvh − 12.75rem on a phone, 24 rem wider up), the strip fades, the live map arrives, focus moves to Shrink map.
  2. Full screen keeps its icon and its link to the graph route with this person in the middle.
  3. No server drawing: EgoGraph, stripFan and the ring go; the strip needs only the faces and two counts, from the same reach the explorer uses so they cannot drift.
