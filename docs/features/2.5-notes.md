## 2.5 Notes **[M1]**

- A note belongs to **one contact**, has an optional **title** and a **Markdown body**.
- Notes show author and timestamp; they can be **pinned** to the top of a profile.
- Markdown supports basic formatting, lists, links, and **@mentions** of other
  contacts **[M2] — shipped**: typing `@` in the note field offers the people the note's
  audience may name, the body stores the id-based token, and `note_mention` records the soft
  link. Same parser, resolver and chip as the journal (§2.20.1) — nothing note-specific.
- Notes are individually **shared or private** (see 2.10).
- Full-text searchable (2.9).
- **Removing** **[M3] — shipped**: a note's **author** may always remove it, and a household
  **admin** may remove another member's **shared** note — never a private one, which an admin
  cannot see (2.10, docs/03 §3.7). Everyone else sees no *Remove* on someone else's note, which
  is named by its author (*· Nina*). Removing goes through the held undo window (§2.23) and
  deletes for good once it closes: its mentions go with it and search no longer finds it. It
  waits for a connection — unlike writing a note, it is not kept in the offline outbox
  (§2.18), so the right to remove is checked at the moment of removal. An admin's removal of
  someone else's note is told to the household in *What's new* (§2.11); an author's own is not.
- **Editing** **[M3] — shipped**: a note's **author** — and nobody else — may edit its title
  and body in place, as the journal's *Edit* does (§2.20); an admin may remove a shared note but
  never edit it (docs/03 §3.7). Visibility and the pin are not in the editor. The mentions are
  rebuilt against the note's own audience, as when it was written, so an edit never widens
  access, and a handle that could be several people is asked about (§2.2.3). Search finds the
  new text and no longer the old (§2.9). Like removing, it needs a connection and is not kept in
  the offline outbox (§2.18); its right is checked when the edit lands. Edits are not in
  *What's new* (§2.11).
