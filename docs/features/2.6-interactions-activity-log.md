## 2.6 Interactions / activity log **[M2]**

A lightweight journal of contact touchpoints, distinct from notes.

- An interaction has: a **type** (met in person, call, video, message, letter, other —
  a gift is a gift record on the Gifts card, §2.25, and the former kind *gift* was converted
  into those, §2.25.4), a **date**, an optional **title/summary**, an optional **description**, and
  optional **participants** (other contacts present).
- Rendered on the profile as part of the person's **Activity** (§2.23), interleaved with journal
  entries in one chronological read.
- Powers "**last contacted**" on the profile.
- **Shipped:** the timeline and the *Log interaction* form live in the **Interactions**
  section of the person page. An interaction is about one **subject** and records a **day**
  (not a time); participants are chosen from the people the member can see, and the subject
  cannot be their own participant. Visibility follows the child-record rule (§2.10): a
  private interaction is only ever listed to its author, and a participant the viewer may
  not see is left out rather than named. Its author can remove it, and so can a household admin when it is shared (§2.10, docs/03 §3.7) — held for the undo window (§2.23), and logged for the household when someone other than the author does it (§2.11).
  **Editing.** Its author alone may correct it in place (docs/03 §3.7): the day, the kind,
  the title, the description and who took part. An admin gets *Remove* on another member's
  shared touchpoint, never *Edit*. Who sees it stays as it was — the editor has no
  shared/private switch, as with a journal entry (§2.20). A participant the author can no
  longer see stays on it untouched, and one already on it stays though they have been archived
  since; anyone newly named must be someone the picker offers. Like removing, editing is not
  kept on the device (§2.18) — it needs a connection, and the right is checked when the edit
  lands. An edit is not told to the household (§2.11).
  **"Last contacted"** in the profile header is derived from the timeline *as the viewer
  sees it*, so it never betrays a private touchpoint. Logging one also posts to the
  household stream (§2.22.2).
