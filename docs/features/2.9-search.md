## 2.9 Search **[M1]**

- **Global search** (keyboard-accessible, `/` or ⌘K) across contact names, descriptions,
  contact-field values, tags, and note bodies. A person is also found by their job title and
  company (§2.2) — on the search page, in ⌘K and the home search field, in every person picker
  and in the People list's find-as-you-type.
- Backed by SQLite **FTS5**; results grouped by type (contacts, notes, gifts, to-dos, moments) with snippets. A
  person found shows their photo, or their initials when they have none.
- **Gifts, on the search page only.** The full search page (`/search`) also matches a gift's
  title and note — not its link, not its occasion — and lists the hits as their own group
  *Gifts* (*Geschenke*) after people and notes. Each hit reads its title, *for* the person it
  is for and where it stands — *Idea*, *Given on 24 December 2025*, *Received on …* — and leads
  to that person's Gifts card (§2.25). ⌘K and Home's search field stay name-only; their
  *Search everything* row opens the page that shows the gifts too. A private gift is found only
  by whoever noted it, a gift on a person the viewer cannot see never (`childRecordVisibleTo`).
- **To-dos, on the search page only.** `/search` also matches a to-do's line and lists the
  hits as *To-dos* (*Todos*) after the gifts: the line, *for* the person it is about, an open
  one first; a done one stays findable, struck through and marked *done* with the day it was
  ticked (§2.26.7). Each leads to the person's To do card. Visibility as for gifts.
- **Moments, on the search page only.** `/search` also matches a moment's title, its text and
  the current names of the people it mentions, and lists the hits last as *Moments*
  (*Momente*): the title, the day and the person whose journal it is in, and a snippet; a hit
  opens that journal. A tag is a word of the text, so `hike` finds a moment tagged `#hike`. A
  query that is **one `#tag`** asks for the tag itself: only the moments carrying it are listed,
  each opening its journal filtered by the tag, under one head row *All 12 moments with #hike*
  that opens the tag's page (the count is the household's, past the search's own limit); nothing
  found says *No moment carries
  #hike* without the offer to add a person (§2.20.2). A private moment is found by its author
  only, its snippet and title blurred like a private note's.
- Respects visibility — private records only appear for their owner.
- **A private note's snippet is blurred** for its author, and its title kept back, while they
  blur their private entries (§2.10.1); the hit, its person and its *Private* mark stay, and
  the header lock shows it in place.
- **Found by a former name, said so.** A person matched by their former name rather than the
  name they are shown by reads *Franziska Abab (formerly Widmer)* — *(früher Widmer)* — muted,
  on the search page, in ⌘K and the home search field, in every person picker and in the People
  list's find-as-you-type; when the shown name matches too, nothing is added. One pure rule
  decides it everywhere (`foundByFormerName`, `src/lib/people/former-name.ts`), folding case and
  accents like the rest of the matching. The @-picker matches names only, so it never needs to.
- **Found by the job, said so.** A row that some word of the query matches only in the job
  title or company — in none of the names and not in the description — carries a small *Job*
  (*Beruf*) tag after the name, so the hit is not a puzzle; every row with a job shows it on its
  own line anyway (§2.2). Same places and the same folding as the former name, one pure rule
  (`foundByJob`, `src/lib/people/job.ts`).
- **Nothing found is an offer.** A search with no results says so and offers *Add "…"*, which
  opens the *Add person* form with the query as the name (§2.2) — the People directory's
  filter does the same when nobody matches (not in the archive, where it would mean nothing).
