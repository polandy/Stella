## 2.24 A person's photos, from Immich **[M3]**

The household's photos live in [Immich](https://immich.app), and Immich already knows who is in
them. Stella learns **which Immich person a contact is**, so the person page can say how many
photos Immich has of them, show a glimpse of the latest, and take anyone there in one tap.
Stella does not become a photo app: Immich stays where photos are kept and browsed. The design
and the decisions behind it are in [docs/concepts/immich.md](../concepts/immich.md); this
section describes what is built (slices 1, 2, 3 and 5: *Connect + link*, *Glimpse*,
*Matching list* and *Use as photo*).

**Optional.** Without `IMMICH_URL` (docs/install.md) nothing of this appears anywhere — no menu,
no Settings section, no route that answers.

### 2.24.1 The connection

One household key, created by the admin in their **own** Immich account with read scopes only
(`user.read`, `person.read`, `person.statistics`, `asset.read`, `asset.view`). The key never reaches a browser and is never
logged. Stella needs **Immich 3.2 or newer**.

*Settings → Immich* shows every member one line:

- **Connected** — *Connected to Anna's Immich · 3.2.4*, and where to link people from.
- **What is wrong**, in words that say what to fix: Immich did not answer; the key was refused
  (deleted or mistyped); the key lacks a scope (*The key cannot read people — it needs
  person.read*); the server is older than 3.2. The two photo scopes are tried on the first
  person's newest photo — listing it, then its smallest thumbnail — so a library whose first
  person has no photo cannot say whether `asset.view` is missing.

The admin also reads what sharing the key means: **everyone in the household can see the photos
of the people linked from this library.** The answer is kept for five minutes (thirty seconds
after a failure), so opening pages does not turn into a call to Immich each time.

### 2.24.2 Linking a person

The Photos card on a person's page gets a small **Immich** menu:

- **Find in Immich** (unlinked) opens a picker of Immich faces, searched by the person's first
  and last name. When the full name finds nobody, the first name is tried. The search can be
  changed, and an empty search lists the library's named people. Unnamed and hidden people are
  never offered. A tap on a face links it.
- **Unlink from Immich** (linked) removes the link. It needs nothing from Immich, so it works
  while Immich is down.

**One Immich person is one contact.** A face already linked to someone else stays in the
picker, greyed out, with *Linked to Bert Example* — or *Linked to someone else* when the member
may not see that person — and cannot be picked. A link to it that arrives anyway (an old picker,
or two members at the same moment) is refused with the same words. Two cards for one face are a
duplicate to merge, not two people.

**Any member** who can see the person may link or unlink them — a link is household data, like
a relationship (concept §9.4). A link has no visibility of its own: it follows the contact, so a
private contact's link is seen only by those who see the contact (§2.10). Linking checks the
person in Immich first, so a person deleted or hidden there since cannot be linked. Setting and
removing a link are written to the activity log (`entity_type = 'immich_link'`, not shown in the
stream).

### 2.24.3 On the person page

Under the gallery, a linked person has one line, filled in after the page has loaded — a slow or
absent Immich never holds the page up:

- **In Immich · 1,284 photos**. When the key cannot count photos, just *In Immich*.
- **Open in Immich** — for **every member** who can see the person, whenever Immich answered for
  them; Stella compares no emails and does not wait for the connection check. It points at
  `IMMICH_PUBLIC_URL/people/{id}` and opens in a new tab. Immich's web links show the photos only
  to someone signed into the account the key belongs to: anyone else lands on Immich's sign-in or
  an empty page. That is accepted on purpose (concept §9.2).
- **This person is no longer in Immich — Unlink?** when Immich no longer has the person.
- **Immich didn't answer.** when it is down, or no longer takes the key; Settings names the cause.

Below the line, **one horizontal strip of their latest twelve photos**, newest first — a
glimpse, not a gallery. It is asked for after the page has loaded and holds placeholder tiles
until it arrives. **Show more** at its end adds the next twelve, as often as there are more;
there is no grid and no paging beyond that. Only photos in Immich's timeline are shown: never an
archived, locked, hidden or trashed one, and no videos. When Immich does not answer, no longer
has the person, or the key may not read photos, the strip is simply not there — the line above
already says what is wrong.

A tap on a photo opens it in the **lightbox** (§2.14), at Immich's `preview` size: its date,
*3 of 12*, left and right — buttons or arrow keys, wrapping at either end — through the strip,
and **Open in Immich** to that photo there, for every member, as on the line. It works for
everyone, whatever their own Immich account, because Stella fetches the picture. **Use as photo**
there copies a square of it into the person's own photos (§2.24.6); nothing else in it changes
anything, and the photo itself stays Immich's.

Offline (§2.18) none of this is shown, and nothing from Immich — no face, no photo, not the
strip's list — is kept on the device.

### 2.24.4 Faces and photos, through Stella

A browser cannot fetch an Immich image itself: an `<img>` cannot carry the key, and the key must
not leave the server. Every image from Immich — a face in the picker, a photo in the strip or the
lightbox — comes from **one signed proxy**, `/media/immich/{token}`:

- **Signed.** Stella hands out an image URL only after the access layer has let the member see
  the person, and signs what it names: the person, their Immich person, the photo and its size
  (or, for a face, the person the picker is for and the face), and an expiry a day out
  (HMAC-SHA256 with `SESSION_SECRET`). The proxy never takes a bare Immich id, so guessing or
  reusing one gets nothing (concept §5).
- **Checked again on every request**, before Immich is asked anything: the signature and its
  expiry; that the member is signed in and still sees the person; for a photo, that the person
  is still linked to the Immich person it was listed for — an unlink or a relink ends every URL
  issued before it. Each refusal answers 404, so the proxy says nothing about which check failed;
  Immich down answers 502.
- **Never kept.** It answers `Cache-Control: private, no-store`, and the service worker never
  keeps anything under `/media/immich` (concept §9.11).
- **Only ordinary images** pass — JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, never SVG — with `nosniff`.

Faces were served unsigned in slice 1; they moved onto the proxy so that one rule holds for
every image (concept §9.10). The picker's route answers each face with its signed URL. The routes'
answers are decided in `src/lib/server/immich/routes.ts`, and the checks in
`src/lib/server/domain/immich/glimpse.ts`, both tested there.

### 2.24.5 Across the household's data

- **Deleting** a person deletes their link.
- **Merging** two people keeps the merged record's link on the survivor; when both were linked,
  the survivor's link wins. The other one goes with the merged record.
- **Export and restore** (§2.15) carry the link under the person, as
  `immich: { person, linked_by, linked_at }`. A restore refuses a link whose id is not an Immich
  id and says so; a link to a person this household already has linked is left out, like any
  row already there.
- **The demo** (`IMMICH_DEMO=true` with `SEED_DEMO=true`) wires an Immich that lives in memory,
  with faces named like the demo household and the key owned by the demo admin, so the feature
  can be tried — and tested — without a real Immich. Each face has as many photos as its count
  says, dated a day apart back from 1 September 2026; a photo is a tile in a shade of the face's
  colour.

### 2.24.6 Use as photo

**Use as photo** in the lightbox — or a pick under **From Immich** when choosing the person's
picture — is the one way a photo from Immich becomes Stella's: a copy a member makes on purpose,
never a sync (concept §4.3). Any member who sees the person may, as with any new picture of them
(§2.14).

**From the picture.** With Immich on the instance (and Stella online), tapping the person's
picture at the top of their page always opens the chooser (§2.14): *Choose a picture…*, their
group photos when there are any, and **From Immich**. For a linked person that section shows
their latest twelve Immich photos — the strip's own signed list, asked for when the chooser opens
— and a tap opens the cropper on that photo's preview, the same path as below. For a person not
linked yet it says so and offers **Find in Immich**, the face picker; a pick there links them in
place, and the chooser opens again on their photos. Without Immich nothing changes: no group
photos, no chooser. Which sections show is pure (`src/lib/contacts/avatar-chooser.ts`).

Either way:

- The preview is fetched again **through the signed proxy** and opens in the **cropper** (§2.14).
  *Use photo* renders the chosen square in the browser at 512 px with its thumbnail and re-encodes
  it — exactly the path of an uploaded avatar, so no metadata of the preview leaves the browser.
  *Cancel* or Escape keeps nothing.
- The square becomes the person's **new photo**: a shared gallery photo they now wear. A photo
  they wore before drops back into the gallery, and the same toast says so. Nothing ties the copy
  to Immich afterwards: it stays when the photo is deleted in Immich or the person unlinked.
- **Dated by Immich.** The copy is dated by when Immich says the photo was taken — its
  `localDateTime`, the camera's clock where it was taken, else the file's date in UTC (§2.14's
  capture date). The date is signed into the preview's token when the strip is listed, so the
  server takes it from its own signature, not from what a browser sends.
- **Checked on the server.** The browser names the preview by its signed token, never by an Immich
  id. Before anything is stored the server checks, through the same checks as the proxy, that the
  token is its own and unexpired, is a *preview* signed for **this** person, that the member still
  sees them, and that they are still linked to the Immich person the photo was listed for. Immich
  is asked nothing at that point, so no asset the browser names is ever fetched. Every refusal
  reads the same — *This photo is no longer available from Immich* — and a reload shows the page
  as it is now. The rule is `src/lib/server/domain/immich/use-as-photo.ts`, tested there.
- **Not offline.** The strip is not shown offline (§2.24.3), and the copy goes now or fails: it
  is never kept on the device to send later (§2.18).

### 2.24.7 Finding your people

*Settings → Immich → **Find your people*** links many people at once (concept §4.2). It is a
review list in the style of the relationship suggestions (§2.4.1): one row per person, **the
Immich face next to the Stella avatar**, both names, and how many photos Immich has of them.
The face does the real work — you recognise your aunt faster than you read her name.

- **Matching is by name**, in a pure function (`src/lib/immich/match.ts`). Names are folded first:
  case, accents and the German transliteration go, so *Müller*, *Mueller* and *muller* agree, and
  a hyphen or an apostrophe is a space.
  - **Likely**: Immich's name is the person's full name — first and last name, the shown name,
    or nickname and last name.
  - **Maybe**: Immich's name is the first name or the nickname alone; or Stella knows the person
    by a first name only and Immich's name starts with it; or it is the first name with one half
    of a double last name (*Sandra Brunner* for *Sandra Brunner-Keller*).
  - A person with a likely match is not offered their maybes, and a face that is somebody's
    likely match is not offered to anyone else as a maybe.
- **What is never listed**: Immich people who are unnamed or hidden there, faces already linked
  to anyone (also to someone the member cannot see — the face is taken, concept §9.8), and people
  already linked. Only people the member can see are listed, through the access layer; archived
  ones are left out.
- **A likely row** has **Link**, one tap. **Link all likely** at the top links every likely row
  shown, when there are two or more.
- **A maybe asks.** It shows every face it could be side by side, each with its name and count,
  and a tap on one links it. Two Immich people with the same name, or one face that two people in
  Stella share a full name with, are always a maybe — never a one-tap link. **Not now** puts a
  row aside for this visit only; nothing is stored, and the next visit asks again.
- **Linking from the list only ever adds.** It goes through the same use-case as the picker
  (§2.24.2), so every check holds, and a person linked meanwhile — by another member, in another
  tab — keeps that link: the row is refused with *Lena Brunner is linked already.* A refused row
  says why under it; the others are linked all the same.
- A linked row leaves the list without asking Immich for the whole list again. When nothing is
  left the list ends quietly: *That's everyone for now* — name more faces in Immich, then **Look
  again**.

**Any member** may use it — a link is household data (§2.24.2). Immich's people are read a page
of a thousand at a time (`GET /api/people`), and the counts six at a time, after the page has
opened: the rows arrive when Immich has answered, and when it does not, the page says why
(unreachable, the key refused, or no `person.read`). Faces come through the signed proxy
(§2.24.4), each signed for the row's person. Like every Immich screen, the page is never kept on
the device (§2.18), and without `IMMICH_URL` it does not exist.
