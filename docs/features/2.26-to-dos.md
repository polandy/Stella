## 2.26 To-dos per person **[After 1.0]**

What the household still owes someone — *give the book back*, *ask how the surgery went*,
*send Hilde the recipe* — used to land in a note and be forgotten there, because a note has no
*done* and nothing brings it back. A to-do is a small, tickable line on a person, optionally
with a day, that goes away once done. Decided with the maintainer on 2026-10-31 and built in
slices; this section grows with them (the rest is still in `docs/concepts/to-dos.md` §6).

**Not a task manager.** No projects, priorities, sub-tasks, recurrence, assignee, push or email
— Stella stays a book about people. **No nudges:** only what a member wrote down comes back,
and only on the day they gave it.

### 2.26.1 What a to-do is

A to-do belongs to **one person**, the one it is about (as a gift does, §2.25.1).

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | a short line, required: *Give back the book* |
| **By** | optional day: *by Fri, 6 Nov*. Without one it is open with no day |
| **State** | **open** or **done**; done keeps when and by which member it was ticked |
| **Visibility** | shared by default, private per record (§2.10) |

Who noted it is kept and shown as *noted by …* on another member's to-do. Who should *do* it is
not a field: in a family that is settled at the table, not in the app.

**Who may change what.** Whoever may see a to-do may tick it, rewrite it and remove it — *I
gave the book back* is often said by whoever did. Only its author makes it private, or shared
again. A private to-do is its author's alone, admins included.

### 2.26.2 The To do card

On the person page (`'todos'` in `CONTACT_SECTIONS`).

- **Where it stands.** Right under the identity card **while something is open**, so a to-do is
  the first thing read about somebody exactly as long as there is one. With nothing open it is
  the one-line card at the end of the page (docs/05 §5.5): *To do · Nothing open.* and
  *+ Add*. Saving the first one takes the reader along to the card's new place. The jump bar
  has no link to it: while it matters it is already at the top.
- **Order:** to-dos with a day first, soonest first; then those without, newest first
  (`orderOpenTodos`, `src/lib/todos/todos.ts`).
- **Each row:** a checkbox, the line, its day — *today*, *tomorrow*, *by Fri, 6 Nov*, or
  *was due Mon, 2 Nov*, plainly, as household language rather than a dunning letter — *noted
  by …* and *private*. A tap anywhere on the line ticks it, not only on the box. The day is read
  against the household's today, never the device's clock. A pencil rewrites the line, the day
  and (for its author) the visibility in place; the bin removes it with the undo window every
  removal has (§2.23).
- **Adding:** *+ Add* opens one field, *What?*; *By when?* adds a day (today to start with),
  *No day* takes it away again. Enter saves.
- **Ticking** strikes the line through at once and saves. The toast says *Done: …* and offers
  *Undo*, which opens it again. Once saved the to-do leaves the card.
- **Offline:** noting and ticking are commands (`todo.add`, `todo.tick`) through the outbox
  (§2.18, docs/04 §4.11.2): kept on the device and shown on the card — a noted one as a kept
  row, a ticked one struck through with *not sent yet* — until sent, and listed on Home's
  *Not sent yet* with the rest. A tick only ever moves towards done, so replaying it is
  harmless. Rewriting, unticking and removing change what others may have seen, so they wait
  for Stella (`todo.edit`, `todo.untick`, `todo.remove`).

### 2.26.3 From anywhere

**The command palette** (docs/05 §5.4) offers *To do for …* beside *Gift idea for …*, the same
two-step pick: the field asks *A to-do for whom?*, the list becomes the people alone, and Enter
on one lands on their page with the card's field open (`?todo=new`).

### 2.26.4 Kept with the person

Export and restore carry the to-dos with their person (§2.15); a merge moves them to the person
kept (§2.2).

### 2.26.5 Done ones in Activity

A ticked to-do leaves the card and turns up in the person's **Activity** (§2.23) on the day it
was ticked — the household's day, kept as `done_on` — as *Done* with the check icon, the
member who ticked it and the line beneath: what the household owed and settled is part of
that person's story. It is the story's fourth source, read from the to-do itself; a private
one reaches its author alone, as on the card. Not in the household stream on Home.

- **Not done after all.** Its checkbox stands ticked; a tap opens it again (`todo.untick`, so
  it needs Stella), it leaves Activity and is back on the card. The toast says *Open again: …*
  without *Undo* — ticking again would give it today's day, not its old one.
- **Removing** it from Activity is offered to whoever sees it, with the undo window every
  removal has. Removing deletes the to-do: it is gone from the story too, nothing is kept.
