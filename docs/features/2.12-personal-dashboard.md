## 2.12 Personal dashboard (Home) **[M2]**

The personal landing view — the first thing a member sees after signing in — giving an
at-a-glance overview of recent household life and acting as a launchpad into the details.

> **Current state:** Home is the capture field + household stream of §2.22, with a **rail**
> beside it holding three bands — **To do** (§2.26.6), **Coming up** (§2.13.3) and **On this
> day** (§2.12.1); the rail is absent when all three are. The panel layout underneath is where further summaries (gifts) slot in once
> those exist; "new people", "recent notes" and "your contributions" are covered by the
> stream. A *Quiet lately* band (people nobody had written about in 90 days) was removed:
> most people in a household are reference records, so the nudges were noise (docs/04 §4.9).

- Composed of **panels**, each summarizing one dimension and linking deeper:
  - **Recent activity** — who added or changed what lately (drawn from the activity feed,
    §2.11), attributed to each member.
  - **New people** — contacts recently added to the household.
  - **Recent notes** — the latest notes across visible contacts.
  - **Gifts given** — recently given gift records (§2.25; who gave what to whom).
  - **Upcoming dates** — birthdays/anniversaries coming up (from §2.13).
  - **Your contributions** — quick return to what *this* member recently added or touched.
- **Personal, yet household-aware:** it is *this user's* dashboard (their upcoming dates,
  their contributions highlighted) while still surfacing shared household activity. It
  strictly respects visibility (§2.10) — other members' private records never appear.
- **Drill-down everywhere:** every item links to the relevant contact profile or panel
  (a note → that note on the contact; a gift → the interaction; a person → their profile),
  so the dashboard is a jumping-off point, not a dead end.
- **Powered by existing data** — activity log, contacts, notes, interactions (incl.
  `kind='gift'`), important dates. **No new tables:** it is a read/aggregation view
  assembled in the domain layer (test-first), reusing the access-control scoping.
- **Configurable later [M3]:** which panels appear and their order; compact vs. comfortable
  density.

> Relationship to §2.11: the activity feed is the household-wide change stream; the
> dashboard is the personal home that *composes* that feed with other summaries (new
> people, gifts, notes, dates) into one overview.


### 2.12.1 On this day

A quiet band, **last in Home's rail**: what the household wrote on today's day and month in
earlier years. Not a nudge and not about seeing anyone more often (most people in a household
are reference records) — a reason to enjoy the notebook.

- **What it shows.** The moments and journal entries (§2.20) and touchpoints (§2.6) the reader
  may see whose day falls on today's day and month in an **earlier** year, and the gallery
  photos (§2.14) taken on it by their capture date. Whole years only: grouped as *1 year ago ·
  2025*, newest year first, the last written first within a year. Today's own entries are not a
  memory yet. With Immich set up (§2.24), **any photo of the timeline** taken on the day in an
  earlier year joins them, whoever is in it, back to the oldest photo but not before 1990 — a
  camera that lost its clock dates its photos to 1970 or earlier, which would cost a question
  per year since; the library is open to the household, as beside an upload (§2.24.9).
- **The band.** One row of up to four photo thumbnails — the gallery's first, then Immich's,
  pilled *Immich* — each with its year, then at most
  **three** entries. A row shows up to two faces (the person, then a mentioned person or a
  participant), the first line — a moment's text with its mentions as names, or a touchpoint's
  title, else its kind — and under it the names and the kind. *More from 1 November* leads to
  `/on-this-day` whenever the page holds more than the band, Immich's photos counted.
- **Where a tap lands.** A moment opens on the person's journal page at that entry (it lists
  every entry, the person's page only the latest); a touchpoint opens the person's Activity
  card; a gallery photo their Photos card. An **Immich photo** opens a lightbox over the page:
  the day and time it was taken, *3 of 7*, back and on through the row it was opened from
  (wrapping), and *Open in Immich* — no *Use as photo*, since no person owns it.
- **`/on-this-day`.** Every memory of a day's date, one card per year with that year's photos and
  entries. Each card shows up to **12** Immich photos of its year, and *Show more* adds the next
  12; a year only Immich has gets a card of its own. A day picker steps a day back or on, or jumps to any day up to today (`?day=`); a day
  still to come, or one that does not exist, reads as today. The palette's *On this day* opens
  it.
- **29 February** is remembered on 28 February in a common year, under its own year.
- **Immich comes later and fails soft.** Immich is asked after the page is drawn, so a slow one
  never holds Home up: while it answers, a band that stands anyway keeps grey tiles in its place
  and the page a card *Photos from Immich…*; a band with nothing else appears once Immich has
  photos. An Immich that does not answer adds nothing — Settings says why (§2.24.1). Nothing of
  it is kept on the device.
- **Absent when empty**, like *Coming up*. **Private entries** are the author's only and stay
  under the veil (§2.10.1). A person the household **archived** is out of it, as out of every
  Home band; a person who **died** stays — remembering them is the point.
- **How.** The use-case `memoriesOn(deps, viewer, day)` reads the three kinds in parallel,
  each one query on a generated `MM-DD` column with its own index (docs/03 §3.3), scoped like
  the stream (`childRecordVisibleTo`). The day comes from the injected clock at the edge; the
  29 February rule, the grouping and the band's cap are the pure `src/lib/memories/`. Immich's
  photos are `immichMemoriesOn` (`domain/memories/immich.ts`), apart from `memoriesOn`: one
  question for the oldest photo of the timeline, then one per earlier year back to it, never
  before `IMMICH_MEMORY_FIRST_YEAR` (1990; *Show more* for an older year answers nothing), all
  at once, each a
  window around the day read on the camera's clock (as the library picker's, §2.24.9), each
  photo signed for the household. The browser asks `GET /immich/on-this-day?day=` (and
  `&year=&from=` for *Show more*), never cached; folding the answer into the band and the cards
  is `src/lib/memories/immich.ts`.
