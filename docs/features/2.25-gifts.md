## 2.25 Gift ideas and gifts given **[M3]**

Two things are hard to remember and annoying to get wrong: what the household gave whom, and
when — so the same book does not arrive twice — and a good idea the moment it comes, so it is
there on the birthday it was meant for. Decided with the maintainer on 2026-10-06 and built in
three slices (#316, #329, and the hints and the palette entry of §2.25.5).

### 2.25.1 What a gift is

A gift belongs to **one person**, the one it is for (a couple's present is noted on each).

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | a short title, required: *Teapot, cast iron* |
| **Note** | optional: size, colour, where they saw it |
| **Link** | optional web address — the shop page. Only `http(s)` is kept; an address pasted without its scheme is read as `https://`; anything else (`javascript:`, `mailto:`) is refused |
| **State** | **idea**, **given**, or **received** (a present from them to the household) |
| **On** | the day a given or received gift changed hands; an idea has none |
| **Occasion** | optional: *Birthday*, *Christmas*, *Anniversary* — stored as a key and shown in the reader's language — or free text, shown as typed |
| **Visibility** | shared by default, private per record (§2.10) |

An idea and the gift it became are **the same record**: *Mark as given* sets the state and asks
only for the day and the occasion; title, note and link stay. A received gift is noted directly,
never from an idea. Who noted it is kept and shown as *noted by*; who *gave* it is not a field.

**Who may change what.** Whoever may see a gift may rewrite it, mark it given and remove it —
a present is the household's, and the member who buys the teapot is rarely the one who noted
it. Only its author may make it private, or shared again, so nobody can hide a gift from the
member who wrote it down.

### 2.25.2 The Gifts card

On the person page, after *Notes* and before *Mentioned in* (`'gifts'` in `CONTACT_SECTIONS`),
with its own jump-bar link.

- **With nothing noted** the card is one line (docs/05 §5.5): *Gifts · No gift ideas yet.*,
  *+ Idea*, and the ⋯ menu holding *+ Given* and *+ Received*.
- **Tabs:** *Ideas* (open ideas, newest first; the tab carries their count, *Ideas · 3*),
  *Given* (newest day first, under uppercase year dividers) and *Received* (the same — the tab
  shows only once there is one). An empty Ideas tab invites: *Got an idea for Hilde? Note it
  here, and it is here when the day comes.* The card header has no count, so its jump-bar link
  has none either.
- **Adding:** *+ Idea* asks for the title; note and link fold away behind *Note or link*.
  *+ Given* (beside it) and *+ Received* (in the ⋯ menu) ask for the day (today by default)
  and the occasion as well. Saving needs only the title, and the day where there is one.
- **Already given.** While the title is typed — adding an idea or a given gift, or rewriting
  one — a title that looks like a gift already **given** to this person says so under the
  field: *Already given: “Teapot, cast iron” on 12 October 2023 (Birthday).* It looks like one
  when either title holds the other, case and accents folded (*teekanne* finds *Teekanne aus
  Gusseisen*), from three letters on; of several, the latest is named; the gift being rewritten
  never finds itself. Ideas and received gifts are not looked at, and nothing is asked of
  Stella — the card already holds every gift the reader may see (`alreadyGiven`,
  `src/lib/gifts/already-given.ts`). It is a hint, not a block: the second tin of her favourite
  tea can be on purpose.
- **Each row:** the title, its day and occasion, the note, *noted by …*, the link, *private*.
  An idea has no day of its own, so it says when it was noted instead — *Added 3 October 2026*,
  from the record's creation time, as the reader's calendar day. An idea offers *Mark as given…*, which opens the day and the occasion; every gift can be rewritten in place (pencil) and removed with
  the undo window every removal has (§2.23).
- **Offline:** noting a gift is a command (`gift.add`) and goes through the outbox like every
  addition (§2.18, docs/04 §4.11.2) — kept on the device and shown on the card until sent.
  Rewriting, giving and removing (`gift.edit`, `gift.markGiven`, `gift.remove`) change what
  others may have seen, so they wait for Stella.

### 2.25.3 In the story

A given or received gift appears in the person's story (*Activity*, §2.23) on its day,
labelled *Given* or *Received*, with the gift icon and `--kind-gift`, its title linking to the
Gifts card and its occasion below. It is **read from the gift record**, a third source of the
merge, so editing or removing the gift changes the story too. Ideas never appear there, and the
story offers no removal for a gift — that happens on the card.

There is no touchpoint kind *Gift* any more (§2.25.4): the gift icon and `--kind-gift` belong
to the gift record alone, and *Log contact* offers *+ Given* on the Gifts card in its place.

### 2.25.4 Gifts from before

Before the gift record, gifts lived in two other shapes. Both become gift records once, so there
is one place for gifts:

- **The Monica gift notes.** The import wrote each Monica gift as a note titled *Gift* /
  *Geschenk* (id `<source>:gift:<monica id>`), its first line `🎁 **Title** — status, day`, then
  the comment and the link. Such a note becomes a gift: Monica's `idea` an idea, `offered`
  given, `received` received. The title, day, comment (as the note) and link stay — the link
  only when it is a web address, read the way every gift link is (§2.25.1); otherwise it stays
  in the note. The visibility, author and the note's own creation time stay too, and the gift
  keeps the note's id. Then the note goes.
- **The gift touchpoints.** Each touchpoint of the kind *gift* becomes a **given** gift on its
  person, on its day, keeping its visibility, author and creation time. Its title becomes the
  gift's title and its description the gift's note; with no title the description is the
  title, with neither the gift is called *Gift* / *Geschenk* in its author's language. A
  touchpoint with participants becomes one gift per person (a gift belongs to one person). Then
  the touchpoint goes, and the kind with it.
- **Nothing written by hand is lost.** A gift note somebody edited since the import, one whose
  first line no longer reads as the import wrote it, and one Stella cannot make a gift of
  (given or received without a day, a status Monica does not have) is left exactly as it is and
  named in the server log (docs/07 §7.10). An original is removed only together with the gifts
  made from it, in one transaction.
- **When.** At every start, after the database migrations, and after every restore. Once nothing
  is left to convert, it writes nothing; the notes it leaves alone are named again at each start.
  Re-running is harmless: a gift takes its original's id (a participant's gift
  `<touchpoint id>:<person id>`), so an original that comes back — from an older archive, say —
  finds its gift already there and is only removed.
- **The story** shows the converted gifts on their old days, as *Given* or *Received* (§2.25.3);
  ideas never appear there.
- **Search.** A gift that was a note leaves the note index and is found as a gift instead, by
  its title and note, on the full search page (§2.9, §2.25.5).
- **The importer** writes Monica's gifts as gift records from now on, under the same ids. One it
  cannot make a gift of stays the note it always was; one whose earlier note was kept because
  somebody wrote in it is not written again.
- **Restore.** An older archive that still holds gift notes or gift touchpoints restores them as
  they are and the same conversion runs straight after, so it lands as gift records; the
  restore's report says how many gifts it made (§2.15).

### 2.25.5 Before an occasion, and from anywhere

- **Coming up.** A person in Home's *Coming up* band (§2.13.3) with open ideas the reader may
  see shows a third line under their row: the gift icon and *2 ideas*, a link to their Gifts
  card. Nothing new is sent or scheduled; the hint only stands where the occasion already does.
  The counts are one scoped query for the people in the band (`countOpenIdeas`).
- **The command palette** (docs/05 §5.4) offers *Gift idea for …* next to *Write a moment*.
  It does not leave the palette: the field empties and asks *Gift idea for whom?*, the list
  becomes the people alone, and Enter on one lands on their page with the Gifts card's idea
  form open and the cursor in *What?* (`?gift=idea`). Backspace in the empty
  field, or the back arrow in front of it, returns to the palette's start. The idea is saved
  like any other — through the outbox when Stella is out of reach.
- **The search page** (§2.9) finds a gift by its title or its note, in a group of its own, and
  each hit leads to the person's Gifts card. ⌘K and Home's search field do not look at gifts.

### 2.25.6 Not planned

A household-wide list of all open ideas (*Christmas shopping list*) waits until the person page
has been lived with. Prices and budgets, wish lists the person shares themselves, and email
reminders are not planned.
